RemindMeds is a multi-platform PWA that helps users manage their medication schedules. It allows users to set reminders for taking their medications, track their adherence, and receive notifications when it's time to take their next dose. As well as keep track of the remaining supply of their medications and notify them when it's time to refill their prescriptions. The app is designed to be user-friendly and accessible on various devices, including smartphones, tablets, and desktops.


## The stack
RemindMeds is currently a progressively built web application (PWA) that utilizes a modern tech stack including
Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase for the backend and Vercel for deployment.

## MVP scope
The MVP (Minimum Viable Product) for RemindMeds will focus on the core functionalities that provide the most value to users. The scope of the MVP includes:

- User Registration and Authentication: Users can create an account, log in, and manage their profile securely.
- Medication Management: Users can add, edit, and delete their medications, including details such as dosage, frequency, and special instructions.
- Reminder Notifications: Users can set up reminders for each medication, and the app will send notifications
at the specified times to ensure they take their medications on schedule.
- Adherence Tracking: The app will track users' adherence to their medication schedules, allowing them to view their history and progress over time.
- Refill Notifications: Users will receive notifications when their medication supply is running low, prompting them to refill their prescriptions in a timely manner.
